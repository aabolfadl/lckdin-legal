# Privacy Policy

*Last updated 16-10-2026*

Lckdin is an offline training log. This policy describes the only data the app handles, the only two ways anything leaves your device, and how the optional Apple Health integration works.

### What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-up, no backend server and no analytics or crash-reporting service. We cannot see your workouts, your measurements, your photos or how you use the app, because none of it is ever sent to us.

### Where your data lives

Everything you log is stored in a database inside this app on your phone. Progress photos are kept in the app’s private storage and are never added to your camera roll. If you uninstall the app, that data is deleted with it — we hold no copy and cannot restore it. Use Settings → Export to keep a backup.

### Advertising

The Progress tab shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. Google receives your device’s advertising identifier and standard technical information such as device type, operating system and approximate region derived from your IP address. Google does not receive any of your training data. We request non-personalised ads only, and we send no information about you — the ad request carries a fixed list of fitness-related topics that is identical for every person using the app. You can review or change your ad choices at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad settings.

### Subscriptions

Lckdin Pro is sold and processed entirely by Apple or Google. Payment details never reach this app and we never see them. The app asks the store whether your subscription is active and stores only that answer on your device.

### Apple Health

On iPhone you can choose to connect Lckdin to Apple Health in Settings → Apple Health. It is off unless you turn it on. When it is on, Lckdin reads your bodyweight, height, body fat, waist, resting heart rate, calories eaten and sleep from Health, and writes your workouts and the bodyweight, height, body fat and waist you log in Lckdin to Health, where workouts also appear in the Fitness app. This exchange happens on your device, between Lckdin and the Health app. Lckdin never sends Health data to us or to anyone else, never uses it for advertising, and never shares it with Google or any other third party. Once data is in Health, Apple manages it under your Health settings, including iCloud sync if you have it turned on. You can stop the exchange at any time by turning the setting off, change what Lckdin may read or write in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → Lckdin, and remove what Lckdin added from there.

### Children

Lckdin is not directed at children and we do not knowingly handle data from anyone under 13.

### Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete. You have direct control: export your data at any time, delete individual records, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

### Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version ships with the app and the date above changes. Questions go to the feedback address in Settings → About.
